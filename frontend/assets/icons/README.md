# Solar linear icons

Sixteen original Solar SVGs by **480 Design**, obtained through Better Design's
Iconify-backed installer on 7 October 2026. Original paths are unchanged;
the app applies theme colors and scaling at render time.

The bell icon was added through the same installer on 8 October 2026.
The outlined Solar leaf was removed on 13 October at the user's request. Every
leaf icon now renders Unlabel's filled brand vector through `BrandLeaf`; dietary
and allergen pictograms retain their distinct food symbols.

## Food and allergen pictograms

On 9 October 2026, 19 project-authored SVGs named `food-*-linear.svg` were added
for the eight dietary preferences and twelve allergen reminders. Solar's
existing `dumbbell-linear.svg` represents higher protein; the other meanings
use individually drawn food silhouettes because the stock Solar set does not
include them. The vectors share its 24-unit viewBox, 1.5-unit rounded strokes
and theme-controlled color. They are original Unlabel artwork, not Solar or
another third-party icon pack, and are kept outside the Solar `.library.json`.

The editor and selected summary use the same symbol for the same choice.
Different choices use distinct assets: sugar cube with down arrow, wheat ear,
carrot, dumbbell, salt shaker, oil drop, crossed sugar spoon, seedling, milk
carton, bread, egg, peanut, walnut, soybean pod, fish, shrimp, sesame seeds,
mustard seed jar, celery stalks and a preservative flask. The visible labels
carry each meaning; these symbols are decorative and make no food-safety claim.
