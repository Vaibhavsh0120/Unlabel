# Retired external product provider

The operator removed NutriGraphAPI on 22 November 2026. Its refreshed key returned
404 on the supplied `/api/lookup` endpoint and 401 on the documented `/fetch`
alias. The adapter, route, client display, settings and secrets were removed.
Historical release contracts remain in Git; they are not current capabilities.

Current results use the licensed catalogue, research, Open Food Facts, community
and separate machine observations. See [optional sources](OPTIONAL_SOURCES.md),
[Product Info](PRODUCT_INFO.md) and [AI routing](AI_ROUTING.md).
