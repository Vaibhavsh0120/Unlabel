additive names and technological functions. It is partial coverage, not an
Indian additive-permission database, allergy assessment or ingredient safety score.

Official indexed page text identifies these names/codes/functions. Direct page
fetches returned 403 during this review; no fresh regulatory permission,
complete current GSFA provisions or FSSAI legal determination is claimed.
The stored short explanations and Hindi translations are project-authored
paraphrases, with primary-source attribution retained. No bulk source document,
health-risk classification or third-party prose is redistributed.

The bounded tracked [JSON projection](../../backend/app/reports/glossary-v1.json)
contains source IDs/URLs, review date and English/Hindi aliases. Runtime validates
known unique sources, codes, dates and schema, then reports its exact byte/hash
artifact. It does not fetch sources during analysis. Unavailable knowledge keeps
the independent report usable.

Matching retains original text spans. Only supported names and explicit INS/E
notations match; bare numbers, partial words and unsupported subtypes remain
unknown. Original ingredient sections remain visible, including unmatched terms.
INS 322 without a subtype does not establish INS 322(i), and a name/function
match does not determine the ingredient's origin or whether the packet is safe
for a person with an allergy. Expand entries only through reviewed sources and
measured unknowns from Indian labels.
