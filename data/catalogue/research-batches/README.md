# Reviewed packet research updates

Use [the template](template.json) for future requests such as researching Maggi,
Kurkure, Lay's or biscuits. Its placeholders are deliberately invalid. Fill them
from the actual packet/manufacturer page; do not guess ingredients or barcodes.
See [the catalogue guide](../../../docs/architecture/CATALOGUE.md#reviewed-research-updates)
for preview, publication and recovery.

Each published directory is named by the active observation snapshot hash. Its
request, plan, manifest, compressed observations and image descriptors are public
source artifacts. `current.json` selects the most recently published snapshot.
New reviewed findings replace current facts for the exact product; previous
snapshots are recovery history. Do not edit the original `popular-india-v1` bundle.
